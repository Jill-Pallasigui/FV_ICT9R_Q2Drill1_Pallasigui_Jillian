Beginner's Guide to Volleyball



Rules in Volleyball

    • 2 teams play on opposite sides of a net.

    • Each team can have up to 3 touches before sending the ball over the net.

    • The ball can be touched with any part of the body, but it cannot be caught or held.

    • If the ball lands out of bounds or a team commits a fault, the other team gets the point.
